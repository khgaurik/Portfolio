My Journey

Three years at Mount Royal University, and how my goals have grown along the way.

Year 1 | 2024 – 2025

Building the foundation

Goal: Aim to build sustainable learning habits and focus on my major and become a programmer.

I began my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a simple plan: learn to code well. My first year covered programming, problem solving, and the mathematics behind it, including calculus, linear algebra, and discrete math, all by the book. I found that I enjoyed the logic and the challenge of turning an idea into working software.

  • Learned core programming and problem-solving skills
  • Built a strong base in mathematics and logic
  • Earned a place on the dean’s and president’s honour roll

Year 2 | 2025 – 2026

Reinforcing Learning

Goal: Go deeper into how machines work, leaning into extracurricular learning.

I thoroughly enjoyed exploring the hardware that legacy and modern machines are built on, refocusing my attention on the machine itself alongside the software. As I moved into building computers, whether in simulations or on physical breadboards, I was fascinated by the human ingenuity behind them, from the initial design to the optimizations that produced powerful computers the size of a fingernail. It nudged me to explore science beyond computers.

  • Led a team of three to build Chuck: The Game
  • Built AlfredAI, a web app that uses the Gemini API and Firebase, as team lead
  • Began mentoring youth as a leader at Bethany Chapel
  • Became more active with Power to Change

Year 3 | 2026 – Present Current year

Exploring Passions and Challenging Self

Goal: Learn as much as I can and explore my potential.

This year I began learning French and enrolled in physics, following my curiosity across disciplines. I also joined the leadership team of MRU Power to Change. Alongside my studies, I’ve started investing in my physical and mental well-being through focused workouts, nutrition, and professional guidance, so I can sustain my growth and realize my potential. I’ve grown a lot academically and personally, and I’m not finished.

  • Started a minor in French and Francophone Studies
  • Began my cognate in Physics
  • Joined the leadership team of MRU Power to Change
  • Started builting my own software, expanding skillbase

After graduation | Spring 2029

Looking ahead

Goal: Make a lasting impact on the world.

I plan to keep this pattern going by picking up something new every year, whether that means exploring art forms, mastering musical instruments, or taking on challenges outside my current skill set. I intend to push my limits, both physical and cognitive, and build a life that is truly my own. Professionally, I hope to build software that helps people, and in everything I do, I want to help as many people as I can, even if each act seems small.
